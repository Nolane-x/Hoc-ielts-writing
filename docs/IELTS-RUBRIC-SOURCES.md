# IELTS Writing rubric references

WMR uses official IELTS public materials to ground task types and criterion names. The in-app Vietnamese guide is an original learner-oriented summary, not an official descriptor reproduction or scoring calibration.

- IELTS Writing resources and criteria: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/writing-test-resources
- Official public Writing Band Descriptors (public version notes update May 2023): https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf
- Official key assessment criteria: https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-writing-key-assessment-criteria.pdf
- Academic Writing format: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing
- General Training Writing format: https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-general-training-test/ielts-general-training-format-writing

Implementation rules: Task 1 uses Task Achievement; Task 2 uses Task Response. Task 2 carries twice the weight of Task 1 in the full Writing score, but WMR assesses one selected task at a time and does not claim a single-task result is the overall Writing band. The built-in guide paraphrases common signals at bands 5–9; it is not a verbatim reproduction. AI estimates are formative and not calibrated against examiner-rated essays.
